Glow Up Diary

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Glow Up Diary (the "App") is developed and published by Abdul Ahad, operating as Undercover Mysteries ("we", "us" or "our"), based in Pakistan. This policy explains what the App does with your information. It applies to version 2.0 of the App and later.

The short version
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your photos, weight, mood and notes are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them.
  • The only exception is Share my journey, which is off unless you turn it on, requires you to be 18 or older, and only ever uploads selected front photos. You can turn it off at any time, and the shared photos are deleted.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Everything you create in the App stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your daily photos (front, side and back).
  • Weight, mood and notes you log with each day.
  • Your profile: the first name you enter, your goals, your starting weight and your preferred units.
  • App settings, such as your theme and whether the daily reminder is on.

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. We do not run a notification server.

This information is removed when you use Erase all data in the App or delete the App. Because it lives only on your device, it is not included in any backup we hold, and we cannot recover it for you.

2. Permissions the App asks for

  • Camera: to take your daily photos.
  • Photo library (read): only if you choose a photo from your library instead of taking a new one. The App reads only the photo you pick.
  • Photo library (add): only when you choose to save a before-and-after image or film to your Photos.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder.

You can change any of these at any time in iOS Settings → Glow Up Diary.

3. Share my journey (optional, 18+)

During setup, and later in Profile, you can choose to share your journey so real transformations can be featured in our marketing. It is off unless you turn it on, and you must confirm that you are 18 or older and that the photos are of you.

What is shared

  • Your day 1 front photo, and the front photo on days 15, 30, 45, 60, 75 and 90. If you skip a milestone day, the next photo you take stands in for it.
  • The day number and capture date of each shared photo.
  • A record of your consent: when you agreed or withdrew, the App version and the platform.

What is never shared

Your side and back photos, your name, your weight, mood or notes, and every photo that is not a milestone.

Where it goes and how it is used

  • Shared photos are stored in a private storage bucket with Supabase, our hosting provider, in the United States. Only the Glow Up Diary team can access them.
  • They are linked to a random anonymous ID created for this feature, not to your name, Apple ID, email or phone number.
  • They may be shown publicly as before-and-after examples in Glow Up Diary ads, App Store previews and on social media.

Changing your mind

Turn sharing off in Profile at any time. The App then deletes every photo it shared from our storage and stops all new uploads. Your consent record is kept, with the date you withdrew, as proof of what you agreed to and when. Photos that already appeared in published material before you withdrew may take some time to be removed from it.

4. Subscriptions

Glow Up Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase and subscription history for this App, and basic device and app information (such as the App version, iOS version and country). It does not receive your photos or anything you log. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

5. Advertising

If you use the free version, the App shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you can watch to unlock a day. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device information, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), and how you interact with ads. Google uses Apple's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking in iOS. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Glow Up Pro subscribers see no ads, and the App does not request ads for them.

7. How long we keep information

  • Data on your device: until you erase it or delete the App.
  • Shared journey photos: until you turn sharing off, or ask us to delete them.
  • Consent records: kept after withdrawal as a record of your choice.
  • Subscription records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Share my journey is available only to people who confirm they are 18 or older. If you believe a child has shared photos through the App, contact us and we will delete them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Most of your data never leaves your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time. For anything we hold, which is only shared journey photos and consent records, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Shared journey photos are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in a private bucket that only the Glow Up Diary team can access. Photos on your device are protected by your device's own security, including your passcode and Face ID.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what is shared through Share my journey, we will ask for your consent again in the App before it applies to you.
